CUSTOMER INFORMATION · LAST REVIEWED OCTOBER 2026

Vehicle enquiries and reservations

Vehicle availability, specifications, condition, pricing and reservation terms should be confirmed directly with Kazzy Autos. Ask for the terms in writing before making any payment. A reservation or deposit is governed by the written agreement provided for that specific transaction.

Deposits and refunds

Refund eligibility, timelines and any applicable charges depend on the signed purchase or reservation agreement and the circumstances of the transaction. Please request and review those terms before paying. No general refund promise is made by this summary.

Vehicle inspection and handover

Customers are encouraged to inspect a vehicle and clarify its condition, included items and handover arrangements before purchase. Any agreed representations or remedies should be documented in the sale paperwork.

Importation, finance and insurance

These services may involve separate third-party providers, timelines, fees and terms. Ask for a written breakdown of the relevant commitments and refund conditions before proceeding.
